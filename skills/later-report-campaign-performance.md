# Report on campaign performance

Later Influence exposes reporting in two places that look alike and are not: `CampaignPerformance` (`/v1/campaign-performance/*`) is the metrics surface, and `Analytics` (`/v1/reporting-details/*`) is the enrichment surface. Use both.

## Base URL
- `https://api.mavrck.co/v1`.

## Auth
- `api-key` request header. See `authentication/later-authentication.yml`.

## Steps
1. **Post-level metrics** — `getPostMetrics` (`GET /v1/campaign-performance/post-metrics`).
2. **Creator-level metrics** — `getInfluencersMetrics` (`GET /v1/campaign-performance/influencers-metrics`).
3. **Conversions** — `getInfluencersConversions` (`GET /v1/campaign-performance/influencers-conversions`). Conversion attribution depends on tracking links (`getActionTrackingLink`) and conversion tags (`ConversionTags` operations) being set up on the campaign first.
4. **Audience insights** — `getAudienceInsights` (`GET /v1/campaign-performance/audience-insights`) and `getAggregatedAudienceInsights` (`GET /v1/campaign-performance/audience-insights/aggregate`).
5. **Enriched reporting rows** — `GetEnrichCampaignsData` (`GET /v1/reporting-details/campaign-details`), `GetCreatorDetails` (`GET /v1/reporting-details/v2/creators-enrichment`) or `getCreatorDetailsMultiCommunity` (`v3`) across communities, and `GetContentEnrichmentV2` (`GET /v1/reporting-details/v2/content-enrichment`).
6. **Cost** — `GetInfluencerIncentiveCosts` (`GET /v1/reporting-details/influencer-incentive-info`) is what turns engagement numbers into cost-per-outcome.
7. **Reviews** — `GetCampaignReviews` (`GET /v1/reporting-details/reviews`) for product review content generated by the campaign.
8. **Export** — `exportContent` (`POST /v1/export/content`) for the raw rows.

## Version the endpoints, not the API
The reporting surface carries its own inline versions: `creators-enrichment` exists at `v1`, `v2` and `v3` under the same `/v1` API version. Pick the highest one your account returns data for; the older ones are still live and still answer.

## Errors
- **`429` on export** — `exportContent` is the ONLY operation in the entire 687-operation contract that declares a 429, and it means "Priority export slot is busy", a concurrency limit rather than a request-rate limit. Serialize your exports; retry after a delay.
- `400 Missing Parameter` — most reporting operations require a campaign or community scope.
- See `errors/later-problem-types.yml`.

## Notes
- No rate-limit headers exist anywhere, so there is no runtime budget signal to read. See `rate-limits/later-rate-limits.yml`.
- Pagination is `limit`/`offset` with no cursor and, on most list operations, no total count. See `conventions/later-conventions.yml`.
- `backfillAnalyticsServiceData` (`POST /v1/reporting-details/backfill` and `/backfill-cost`) recomputes history; it is expensive and asynchronous.
